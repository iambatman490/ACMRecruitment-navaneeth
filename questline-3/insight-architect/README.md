# Insight Architect - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
1. **Histogram (Math Scores)**: Displays the overall distribution and frequency spread of math scores.
2. **Bar Chart (Parental Education vs. Scores)**: Compares average student performance relative to parents' highest education level.
3. **Box Plot (Test Preparation Course)**: Evaluates the impact of completing test preparation on reading score percentiles and outliers.
4. **Scatter Plot (Reading vs. Writing Scores)**: Shows the direct relationship between reading and writing abilities across genders.
5. **Heatmap (Score Correlation Matrix)**: Quantifies linear correlations across math, reading, and writing metrics.
## Key Insights & Observations
1. **Strong Reading & Writing Correlation**: Reading and writing scores have a near-perfect positive correlation ($r > 0.95$), indicating strong interrelation in language performance.
2. **Impact of Test Preparation**: Students who completed the test preparation course consistently scored higher medians across all assessment categories compared to those who did not.
3. **Parental Education Influence**: Students whose parents hold master's or bachelor's degrees show consistently higher average exam scores than those whose parents completed high school only.
4. **Gender Disparities Across Subjects**: Female students outperformed male students in reading and writing, while male students performed slightly higher on average in math.
5. **Normal Distribution Profile**: The score distributions for math, reading, and writing approximately follow a bell curve centered around the 65–70 point average mark.
6. **Low Outlier Spread**: Scores below 30 are rare and represent extreme outliers, primarily concentrated among students who had standard lunches and no prep courses.
